# Phase I related-work matrix — initial primary-source pass

**Scope:** identify nearby mechanisms that constrain novelty claims. This is not an exhaustive systematic review, and no originality claim is made. Search date: 2026-10-08.

| Work | Mechanism and scheduling | Objective/evidence | Relation to IRCN hypothesis | Boundary / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graves, *Adaptive Computation Time for Recurrent Neural Networks* (ICLR 2016) | Learns variable numbers of recurrent computation steps per input; halting is based on learned recurrent state. | Synthetic algorithmic tasks and character-level language modeling; reports task quality and ponder steps. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08983) | Direct precedent for task-conditioned variable compute in recurrent nets. | Does not itself establish asynchronous per-node graph scheduling or connectome-specific value. Per-step ponder count does not equal wall-clock savings. |
| Campos et al., *Skip RNN: Learning to Skip State Updates in Recurrent Neural Networks* (ICLR 2018) | Learned gate skips recurrent state updates; can include a budget constraint. [Primary paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=HkwVAXyCW) | Evaluated update sparsity with sequence-task performance. | Strong precedent for learned selective recurrent updates. | Main distinction to test is continuous/asynchronous node-level execution and local causal message propagation; that difference is not automatically novel or useful. |
| Wang et al., *SkipNet: Learning Dynamic Routing in Convolutional Networks* (ECCV 2018) | Activation-conditioned gating skips convolutional blocks within a residual network. [Primary paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Xin_Wang_SkipNet_Learning_Dynamic_ECCV_2018_paper.html) | Reports accuracy and computation over image benchmarks. | Establishes learned conditional routing and task-quality/cost tradeoffs as prior art. | Layer/block routing differs from a directed recurrent graph with time-stamped state and asynchronous local messages. |
| Heemels, Johansson & Tabuada, *An Introduction to Event-Triggered and Self-Triggered Control* (CDC 2012) | Event-triggered execution reacts to observed state/error; self-triggered execution predicts a future execution time. [Primary author/institution record](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/an-introduction-to-event-triggered-and-self-triggered-control/) | Control and communication savings are studied with stability/performance guarantees. | Terminology and scheduling distinctions apply directly; avoid calling a sampled threshold policy asynchronous self-triggering unless its next time is predicted. | Control guarantees rely on system assumptions and are not transferrable to a learned neural model without proof. |
| Primozic, *Growing Bonsai Networks with RNNs* (2023, technical blog) | Trains sparse recurrent computational graphs, then prunes/quantizes edges and removes disconnected components; the state updates on discrete timesteps. [Author write-up](https://cprimozic.net/blog/growing-sparse-computational-graphs-with-rnns/) | Demonstrations on small synthetic functions, with engineering and training observations. | Relevant to sparse graph topology and mechanistic graph inspection. | Non-peer-reviewed; static sparsity/pruning is distinct from online state-dependent scheduling. Not evidence for dynamic compute or biological structure. |
| Costa & Santos, *Directed Equilibrium Propagation Revisited* (Mathematics 2025) | Studies directed recurrent dynamics and a local learning rule with leakage and convergence conditions. [Publisher paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/11/1866) | Theoretical convergence analysis and small-network learning results. | Relevant to directed recurrent dynamics and stability, not because it uses the same scheduler. | Training/dynamics contribution; does not demonstrate online selective local execution. |
| Harris & Gong, *Nested spatiotemporal theta–gamma waves organize hierarchical processing across the mouse visual cortex* (Nature Communications, 2026) | Reports nested traveling theta waves, localized gamma packets and phase-locked spikes in mouse visual cortex. [Primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68893-4) | Analysis of Neuropixels recordings during a visual change-detection task; neural signals relate to hierarchy and behavior. | May motivate hypotheses about coupled timescales and spatially localized activity. | Observed neural organization does not show an artificial scheduler saves compute, nor establish that this model is biologically equivalent. |

## Initial synthesis

**Verified facts:** the cited works cover adaptive recurrent step counts, learned recurrent skipping, dynamic routing, event/self-triggered control, sparse recurrent graph construction, directed equilibrium-based learning, and multiscale biological neural dynamics.

**Inference:** the phrase “dynamic computation selection” is too broad to support an originality claim. A plausible differentiator is the conjunction of task-loss counterfactual influence, local graph-constrained asynchronous execution, and a measured budget-aware scheduler. This conjunction needs wider prior-art search, implementation, and falsifiable comparisons before any novelty statement.

**Unresolved:** nearest work on asynchronous non-spiking continuous-valued RNNs, learned per-node self-triggering, sparse event propagation, and counterfactual task-impact ranking has not yet been exhaustively searched. This matrix must be expanded during Phase II before locking a public novelty claim.
